1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY & COMPANY VISION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
UniLink Technologies Inc. is building the unified digital social and academic
operating system for higher education students globally. College students
currently struggle with fragmented communication across dozens of single-purpose
apps (WhatsApp, Discord, LinkedIn, Drive, Slack). UniLink consolidates the entire
student lifecycle into one high-retention, real-time native platform.

By combining real-time project teammate matching, low-latency WebRTC video
broadcasting, instant multimodal AI tutoring (Google Gemini 1.5 Flash), a cashless
P2P barter market, and a gamified trust graph, UniLink establishes a high-frequency
daily habit loop for Gen Z students.

Key Traction & Technical Milestones (Current Native Android MVP):
- Fully Functional Production Codebase (Kotlin, Jetpack Compose, ViewBinding).
- Real-Time Cloud Infrastructure: Google Firebase Cloud Firestore + FCM.
- WebRTC Video Engine: Integrated STUN + TURN relay servers on port 443 for
  instant 1080p/720p live streaming across restrictive campus firewalls and 4G/5G.
- Embedded Generative AI: Gemini 1.5 Flash text and vision engine for instant
  doubt solving (<1 second) and AI project idea generation.


2. THE UNMET MARKET NEED & PROBLEM STATEMENT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Higher education students face severe structural inefficiencies every day:

1. Communication & Campus Noise Fragmentation:
   Students manage 10+ WhatsApp groups, Slack workspaces, and Discord servers.
   Important academic announcements and project discussions get buried.

2. High Friction in Skill-Based Teammate Formation:
   When entering hackathons or capstone projects, students cannot easily discover
   peers with complementary technical skills (e.g. Kotlin developer seeking a
   PyTorch ML engineer or UI/UX designer).

3. Academic Delay & Doubt Bottlenecks:
   Homework and coding doubts take hours or days to solve on traditional forums
   where peers lack real-time incentives to help.

4. Wasteful Campus Micro-Economy:
   Textbooks, calculators, lab gear, and tech accessories sit idle in dorms.
   Students lack a localized, cashless, safe P2P barter market to exchange items.

5. Lack of Verifiable Campus Peer Trust:
   Current platforms lack localized trust verification. Students have no reliable
   way to evaluate a potential teammate's reliability or skill proficiency.


3. DEEP PRODUCT ARCHITECTURE & CORE VALUE PROPOSITION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
UniLink operates as 8 interconnected, high-engagement modules:

[MODULE 1: LAUNCHPAD & PROJECT MATCHING]
- Multi-filter team discovery based on skills, hackathon goals, and status.
- Configurable Team Size Limits (e.g. 2 to 6 students needed).
- One-click "Open Team Chat 💬" syncing all accepted team members into real-time group chat.
- "✨ AI Generate Idea" button powered by Gemini AI generating project titles,
  descriptions, tech stacks, and tags automatically.

[MODULE 2: CAMPUS LIVE STAGE & WEBRTC VIDEO ENGINE]
- Broadcaster & Watcher Modes for live coding sessions, guest lectures, and broadcasts.
- WebRTC P2P encrypted streams with STUN + TURN fallback over TCP/UDP port 443.
- In-call communication audio routing (`AudioManager.MODE_IN_COMMUNICATION`) for
  crystal-clear loudspeaker audio and acoustic echo cancellation.
- Interactive live audience reactions (Floating Emojis) and real-time live chat.

[MODULE 3: UNIAI GENIUS TUTOR & MULTIMODAL VISION]
- Powered by Google Gemini 1.5 Flash API.
- Instant AI Doubt Auto-Solver: Automatically posts step-by-step verified solutions
  to student doubts in <1 second.
- Multimodal Vision: Analyzes photos of whiteboards, circuit diagrams, and handwritten
  equations to generate clear explanations.

[MODULE 4: BARTER BLOCK (CASHLESS PEER MARKET)]
- Cashless peer-to-peer exchange of notes, textbooks, electronics, and gear.
- Real-time Firestore sync with category filters (Notes, Tech, Snacks, Gear).
- 1-Tap "Chat Trader & Swap 💬" opening direct 1-on-1 chats to finalize trades.

[MODULE 5: DOUBT BOARD & GAMIFIED RETENTION LOOP]
- 10-Hour active retention window for academic Q&A.
- Reward coins allocated by OP to incentivize quality peer answers.
- Automated UniCoins payouts upon verified solutions.

[MODULE 6: TRUST GRAPH & LOYALTY STARS ⭐]
- Students grant "Loyalty Stars ⭐" to peers for exceptional teamwork or mentoring.
- Granting a star awards the recipient +1 Loyalty Star, +15 Impact XP, and +10 UniCoins.
- Accumulating 3+ Loyalty Stars unlocks the "⭐ Most Trusted Student" badge on the
  Leaderboard, Search Radar, and Profile.

[MODULE 7: REAL-TIME PROXIMITY RADAR & CAMPUS PULSE]
- Proximity radar discovering nearby active students (with privacy controls & Incognito mode).
- Real-time campus activity feed broadcasting achievements, doubts, and project launches.

[MODULE 8: RESOURCE VAULT]
- Centralized repository for shared past year papers, lab reports, and lecture slides.
