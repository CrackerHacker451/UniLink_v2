Newton's Lobby — Child Safety & CSAE/CSAM Policy

Newton's Lobby (com.Newtons_Lobby.myapp) maintains a strict zero-tolerance policy against Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM), Child Sexual Exploitation and Abuse (CSAE), and any form of minor endangerment.

1. Zero-Tolerance Policy & Prohibited Conduct

We strictly prohibit the creation, uploading, sharing, transmission, or storage of CSAM or any content that exploits, abuses, or endangers children. Any user attempting to share or promote such material will be immediately permanently banned, and their account data will be reported to appropriate law enforcement authorities.

2. Automated & AI-Powered Content Detection

Newton's Lobby utilizes zero-knowledge AI content moderation (0G Private Computer) and automated media scanning to detect, block, and flag inappropriate content, photos, and videos prior to public display.

3. In-App User Reporting & Moderation

Users can report any safety concern, inappropriate behavior, or objectionable content directly inside Newton's Lobby. Reported items are flagged for priority human admin review, and offending users are immediately suspended.

4. Reporting to Authorities (NCMEC)

Newton's Lobby complies with all applicable regional, national, and international child protection laws. Any detected instance of CSAM/CSAE is immediately reported to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) and relevant law enforcement agencies.
