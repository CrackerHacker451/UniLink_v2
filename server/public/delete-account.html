Official Data Safety & Deletion

Newton's Lobby — Account & Data Deletion Request

Newton's Lobby (package name: com.Newtons_Lobby.myapp) respects your privacy rights and provides full control over your account data.

1. In-App Immediate Account Deletion

You can delete your account and all associated data at any time directly inside the Newton's Lobby Android App:

  1. Open the Newton's Lobby App on your phone.
  2. Go to Profile Settings ⚙️ tab.
  3. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete Account & Data 🗑️.
  4. Confirm deletion. Your account, messages, posts, and profile records will be permanently purged immediately.

2. Online Web Deletion Request

If you no longer have access to the mobile app and wish to delete your account data, please send an email request to our designated data protection officer:

Data Safety Email: dtgtgm@gmail.com
Subject Line: Account Deletion Request — [Your Email / User ID]
Processing Time: Requests are processed within 48 hours.

3. Data Types Purged Upon Deletion

  • Firebase Authentication User Credentials
  • Firestore User Profile & UniCoins Balance Records
  • Chat Message History & Uploaded Media Attachments
  • DareDrop Challenge Submissions & Liked Posts
