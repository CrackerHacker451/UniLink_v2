Newton's Lobby — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 21, 2026. Newton's Lobby (com.Newtons_Lobby.myapp) is committed to protecting your privacy and security. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our mobile application and web services.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account Information: Name, university email address, and profile picture provided during sign-up or Google Sign-In.
  • User Content: Study notes, DareDrop video/photo challenge proofs, and chat messages sent within the app.
  • Device & Usage Data: Firebase App Check tokens, notification tokens for FCM push alerts, and location data when enabled for campus radar discovery.

2. World ID Zero-Knowledge Privacy

Newton's Lobby integrates World ID 4.0 IDKit and World ID Agent Pass (https://auth.world.org/). We use zero-knowledge proofs and SHA-256 nullifier hashes to verify unique human status without storing any biometric data or real-world identity files on our servers.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your information strictly to provide campus networking, real-time 1080p video calls, study note sharing, and zero-knowledge AI proof verification. We never sell or lease your personal information to third parties.

4. Data Storage & Security

Your data is secured using Firebase Cloud Storage, Firestore persistent encryption, and Arkiv Network DB-Chains. Heavy files in the Study Vault are encrypted using AES-256 and stored on Swarm decentralized storage.

5. Account Deletion & Data Rights

You can request account deletion at any time directly inside the app under Profile Settings or by emailing our designated privacy officer at dtgtgm@gmail.com. Upon account deletion, all associated user records are permanently purged.

6. Contact Us

For any privacy inquiries or data rights requests, contact us at: dtgtgm@gmail.com
